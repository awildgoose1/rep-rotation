How it works

Nothing is ever locked. Each exercise shows when you last did it: Just done (under 2 days), Recently done (2–4 days), Rested (4–7 days) or Fresh (a week or more, or never). The two stalest exercises in each group are suggested. Working max is the heaviest weight you've logged for an exercise, so it reflects your usual working weight rather than a one-rep max.
